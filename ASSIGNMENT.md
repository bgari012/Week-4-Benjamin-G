# Week 4 Assignment: Animation, Editing & VFX

**Due:** End of Week 4 | **Late policy:** 20% deduction; accepted through end of Week 5

---

## Project: 3-5 Minute Promo or Short Story

Create a finished video showcasing skills from the course.

### Requirements
- **Length:** 3-5 minutes
- **Must include at least 2 of these techniques:**
  - Transitions between clips
  - Split-screen or picture-in-picture
  - Keyframe animation (scale, position, or opacity)
  - Green screen compositing
  - 3D element composited into live footage (Blender VFX)
- **Topic:** Your choice — promo for a fictional product, story about something you made in Weeks 1-3, personal introduction, or creative narrative

### Optional but encouraged
- Feature your physical projects from Weeks 1-3
- Add voiceover or narration

### Export settings
- Format: MP4 (H.264), 1080p recommended

---

## Reflection

Write a **~300-word reflection** using the [reflection template](reflection-template.md).

## Submission Instructions

Upload your video to Google Drive (share link), YouTube (unlisted), or Vimeo.

1. Create `links.md` with your video link
2. Create `reflection.md`
3. Commit and submit repo URL on Canvas

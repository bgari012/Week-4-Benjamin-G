## Session 1 (Day 1): Storyboarding & Non-Linear Editing

**Non-linear editing (NLE)** lets you rearrange, layer, and modify clips in any order without destroying originals.

### DaVinci Resolve (recommended)
Professional-grade, free video editor used in Hollywood.
- Download: [blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve) (free)
- Alternatives: **CapCut** (browser) or **OpenShot** (free desktop)

### Techniques to practice

| Technique | What it does |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Transitions** | Fade, cut, wipe between clips |
| **Split-screen** | Two clips playing side by side |
| **Keyframing** | Animate scale, position, or opacity over time |
| **Green screen keying** | Replace a solid-color background |

**In-class activity:** Using stock footage (or your own clips from Weeks 1-3), create a short promo video.

## Session 2 (Day 2): Blender VFX & Compositing

**Camera tracking:** Blender analyzes camera movement in footage and places your 3D models into the scene so they move with it.

**In-class activity:**
1. Import your Week 3 3D model into Blender
2. Track the camera movement in a short video clip
3. Composite your 3D object into live footage

**Use the 3DXP room or STAR Lab computers** if you need more processing power.
